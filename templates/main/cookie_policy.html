Cookie Policy

What this website stores in your browser, and why

This website uses very few cookies. We do not use advertising cookies, and we do not track you across other websites.

What cookies we use

We set one cookie, a session cookie, which keeps you signed in while you use the site and protects forms against cross-site request forgery. It is strictly necessary for the website to work and expires after 24 hours or when you sign out.

This cookie is marked HttpOnly, meaning scripts cannot read it, and SameSite Lax, meaning it is not sent from other websites.

What we do not use

  • No advertising or retargeting cookies
  • No analytics or visitor tracking cookies
  • No social media tracking pixels

Third party services

Some files on this site are served by other companies. These do not set cookies for us, but they do receive your IP address as part of delivering the file, which is how the web works for any externally hosted resource.

  • Cloudinary, which stores and delivers our package images
  • Google Fonts and Cloudflare, which deliver fonts and icons

Links to our Facebook, Instagram, and TikTok pages are ordinary links. They load nothing until you click them, and no content from those platforms is embedded here.

Managing cookies

You can clear or block cookies in your browser settings. Blocking the session cookie will prevent you from signing in or submitting forms, since the site relies on it to keep you logged in and to validate submissions.
